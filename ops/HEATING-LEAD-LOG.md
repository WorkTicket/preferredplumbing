## Definitions

- **Inquiry:** delivered form or connected call (click-to-call taps stay separate)
- **Qualified heating inquiry:** service, location, project type, and stage fit agreed criteria
- **Estimate:** appointment or issued quote (track separately when possible)
- **Booked project:** accepted job confirmed by the team
- **QA:** labeled test submissions — never count as qualified
